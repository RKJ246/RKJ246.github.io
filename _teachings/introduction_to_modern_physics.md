## Course Overview

This introductory course prepares students in basic principles in electromagnetism, spectroscopy, and nuclear physics.

## Prerequisites

- Basic knowledge of grade 12 physics
- Understanding of basic lab equipments such as screw-gauge, vernier-calliper etc.

## Textbooks

- Primary: "Modern Introductory Physics (second edition)" by an innovative undergraduate physics textbook written by Charles H. Holbrow, James N. Lloyd, Joseph C. Amato, Enrique Galvez, and M. Elizabeth Parks
